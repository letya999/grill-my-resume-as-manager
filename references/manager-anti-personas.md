# Manager anti-personas (resume signals)

Market signal from PM/Project job-search communities: employers stop buying "harness management" and process wrappers. Flag these when the resume *reads* as one of them. Diagnosis only — name the signal with quotes, do not rewrite bullets.

## 1. Project administrator
**What they do in text:** Jira hygiene, status collection, meeting setup, deadline pings, status reports.
**Resume signals:** majority of bullets about tools/process without delivery outcome; "вёл Jira/Confluence"; "собирал статусы"; "напоминал о сроках".
**Why it kills:** looks like expensive ops glue replaceable by automation or the team itself.

## 2. Project proxy (telephone)
**What they do in text:** carry messages customer ↔ eng; "коммуникации", "синхронизация", "эскалации" with no own decisions.
**Resume signals:** no product/domain/tech substance; every outcome attributed to "команда/заказчик"; no trade-offs owned.
**Why it kills:** adds latency and distortion; hiring managers cannot see judgment.

## 3. Ritual agile PM
**What they do in text:** ceremonies as achievements — daily, retro, planning, grooming, review.
**Resume signals:** "внедрил Scrum/Kanban" with no before/after on flow, quality, or business; ceremony list without empirics.
**Why it kills:** cargo cult; process tax without value.

## 4. Unimmersed delivery manager
**What they do in text:** owns dates/end-to-end flow but never shows engineering reality.
**Resume signals:** pure schedule pressure language; no MR/docs/risks grounded in system; blockers only "организационные".
**Why it kills:** pressure without understanding is toxic and fails senior bars.

## 5. Manager-router
**What they do in text:** always "привлёк аналитика / техлида / продакта / CTO".
**Resume signals:** facilitation of others' work as main content; zero personal decisions or owned outcomes.
**Why it kills:** "why is this person in the chain?"

## 6. General management without domain
**What they do in text:** "управляю людьми и процессами" across unrelated industries with no deep domain thread.
**Resume signals:** title soup; no industry vocabulary; achievements portable-empty ("оптимизировал процессы" with no object).
**Why it kills:** market wants T-shaped: breadth + domain depth. Pure generalist sorts last.

## Scoring use
For each persona: **CLEAR / PARTIAL / NONE** with 1–3 verbatim quotes.
If CLEAR on 2+ personas → systemic issue "reads as harness/admin PM", weight heavily in verdict.
